# Tailoring protofolio
Tailoring is a skilled profession that involves designing, cutting, and stitching garments according to individual measurements and preferences. A professional tailor must have a good understanding of fabrics, body measurements, patterns, stitching techniques, and garment finishing. Modern tailoring also combines traditional skills with fashion trends and computer-aided design. With creativity, accuracy, and practice, tailoring can become a successful career and a valuable source of income.
